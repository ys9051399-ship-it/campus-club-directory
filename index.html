About CCA

CCA (Centre for Cognitive Activities) is the premier technical and innovation hub of NIT Durgapur, serving as the driving force behind the institute's scientific, technical, and intellectual ecosystem. It provides a platform where students transform classroom knowledge into practical applications through creativity, collaboration, and innovation. The club organizes a diverse range of technical competitions, coding contests, workshops, seminars, quizzes, hackathons, and interactive sessions that encourage students to think critically, develop problem-solving skills, and explore emerging technologies. By fostering a culture of curiosity and continuous learning, CCA inspires students to challenge conventional thinking and push the boundaries of innovation.

Beyond academics, CCA plays a pivotal role in coordinating and managing many of the institute's flagship technical and extracurricular activities throughout the year. The club brings together students from diverse disciplines, creating an environment that promotes teamwork, leadership, and interdisciplinary collaboration. Through its initiatives, CCA nurtures young innovators, equips them with practical and organizational skills, and empowers them to become confident professionals capable of addressing real-world challenges. With its unwavering commitment to excellence and innovation, CCA continues to be a cornerstone of technical growth and student development at NIT Durgapur.

About ISTE

The Indian Society for Technical Education(ISTE) is the leading National Professional non-profit making Society for the Technical Education System in our country with the motto of Career Development of Teachers and Personality Development of Students and overall development of our Technical Education System operating in association with All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and other International Projects.

ISTE NIT Durgapur took birth on the auspicious occasion of Saraswati Puja Day way back in February, 1995 in our erstwhile called REC Durgapur and is the Oldest Student Chapter of Eastern India. Additionally, ISTE NIT Durgapur nominates projects and papers for the Indian Journal of Technical Education (IJTE) published by the ISTE HQ, New Delhi on quarterly basis with the aim to provide an appropriate platform presenting well considered, meaningful, constructively thought provoking, non-political and non-conventional but critically analysing and synthesizing present and future aspects of the technical education system supported with meaningful suggestions for solutions, refinement and innovation

About SAE

The SAE Collegiate Club of NIT Durgapur is one of the institute's most dynamic technical societies, dedicated to fostering innovation, creativity, and excellence in the field of automotive engineering and emerging technologies. Throughout the year, the club organizes a wide variety of flagship events, hands-on workshops, competitions, and expert lectures that provide students with invaluable practical exposure. Some of its most notable initiatives include F.I.S.T, CrypTech, Software Defined Vehicle (SDV) Workshop, Drag & Drift Competition, and TEDx Talks, each designed to inspire technical curiosity, enhance problem-solving abilities, and bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world applications.

As an integral part of Team Aavishkar, SAE plays a crucial role in planning and managing Aarohan, the second-largest techno-management festival in Eastern India, attracting participants from prestigious institutions across the country. Beyond on-campus activities, the club has established a strong digital presence through platforms such as Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and YouTube, where it regularly shares insightful technology-related content, event highlights, project showcases, and industry updates. By connecting students, professionals, and automobile enthusiasts worldwide, the club continues to build a vibrant community that promotes learning, collaboration, and innovation in the ever-evolving world of engineering.
